Your Guide to Riyadh

Cultural insights and practical tips for a respectful, enjoyable visit

🕌

Prayer Times & Etiquette

  • • 5 daily prayers — Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha
  • • Prayer call (Adhan) heard citywide — a normal part of daily life
  • • Short shop closures during prayer times are expected
  • • Mosque visits: remove shoes, dress modestly
  • • Non-Muslims welcome in most mosques outside prayer times
👔

Dress Code & Modesty

Men

  • • Casual Western clothing acceptable
  • • Shorts OK in malls and gyms
  • • Avoid tank tops in public spaces

Women

  • • Abaya not required for tourists
  • • Cover shoulders and knees in public
  • • Headscarf optional; required in some mosques

More conservative dress at all religious sites.

🤝

Cultural Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • ✓ Greet with "As-salamu alaikum"
  • ✓ Use right hand for eating, greeting, giving
  • ✓ Ask permission before photographing people
  • ✓ Respect prayer times

Don't

  • ✕ Public displays of affection
  • ✕ Photograph government buildings
  • ✕ Eat or drink in public during Ramadan daylight

Money & Tipping Culture

  • • Currency: Saudi Riyal (SAR)
  • • Tipping: 10–15% optional; service charge often included
  • • Small tips: round up or SAR 5–10 for good service
  • • Cash useful for small vendors and markets
  • • Cards widely accepted across malls and restaurants
  • • ATMs abundant with English interface available
🌡️

Weather & Packing Tips

Best Oct – Mar  ·  15–25°C
Hot Apr – Sep  ·  35–45°C

Pack

  • • Sunscreen SPF 50+
  • • Hat and sunglasses
  • • Light, breathable layers
  • • Modest clothing options
  • • Comfortable walking shoes
  • • Reusable water bottle
🏥

Health & Safety

  • • Pharmacies: green cross sign, widely available
  • • Tap water safe — bottled water preferred
  • • Sun protection essential in summer months
  • • Excellent medical care; English-speaking doctors available
  • • Travel insurance strongly recommended
997 Ambulance (emergency)
📅

Ramadan Considerations

Seasonal
  • • Eating and drinking in public restricted during daylight hours
  • • Business hours are shorter across the city
  • • Iftar (sunset meal): vibrant dining experiences
  • • Night activities thrive — Ramadan tents and events
  • • Respect fasting locals in shared public spaces

Check Islamic calendar for current year dates.

SIM cards: Available at the airport arrivals hall from STC, Mobily, and Zain. Purchase on arrival for instant connectivity.
