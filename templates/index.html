About Riyadh

The capital of Saudi Arabia — a city that blends ancient heritage with futuristic ambition. Home to over 7 million people, Riyadh is the cultural and economic heart of the Kingdom.

🕌

Rich Heritage

From Diriyah's UNESCO mud-brick palaces to Al Masmak Fortress, Riyadh's history stretches centuries deep.

🏙️

Modern Skyline

Kingdom Centre Tower and Al Faisaliah define a skyline that rivals any global city, surrounded by world-class malls and entertainment.

🌅

Desert Landscapes

Beyond the city, the Edge of the World offers dramatic escarpment views just 90 minutes from the centre.

Travel Tips

Essential things to know before you visit Riyadh

🕐

Prayer Times

Shops close for 15–30 minutes during each of the 5 daily prayer calls. Plan around this — most apps including Google Maps show prayer times. Peak closures are at Dhuhr (midday) and Maghrib (sunset).

👗

Dress Code

Riyadh is relaxed compared to previous years. Dress modestly in public — covered shoulders and knees. Abaya is no longer required for tourists but is appreciated at heritage sites.

🚕

Getting Around

Uber and Careem are widely available and reliable. The Riyadh Metro (3 lines, 85 stations) is fast and affordable. Avoid walking outdoors in summer — temperatures can reach 48°C.

💳

Money & Payments

Saudi Riyal (SAR) is the local currency. Card payments and Apple/Google Pay are accepted almost everywhere. ATMs are abundant in malls and hotels. Alcohol is not available in Saudi Arabia.
